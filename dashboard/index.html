Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I add a new agent?

Agents are discovered automatically. Deploy the monitoring agent on the endpoint and enrol it with your manager — the platform periodically reads the manager's agent inventory and the new endpoint appears in the Endpoints tab within about a minute. No manual entry in the dashboard is required.

Q: What does a "highly anomalous" verdict mean?

Every event is scored from 0 to 1 by the three-layer engine (isolation forest, per-user autoencoders, and the FAISS similarity model). A score of 0.75 or above raises an alert, and the verdict escalates suspicious → anomalous → highly anomalous as the score climbs. "Highly anomalous" is the top band: a strong deviation from that user's normal baseline across several signals — these should be triaged promptly.

Q: How often does the dashboard refresh?

New alerts stream in live over a persistent connection (Server-Sent Events); the aggregate panels (risk meter, user/agent metrics, campaigns) reconcile every 60 seconds. Use the manual Refresh control to force an immediate pull, and the timeline buttons (24H default) to change the window.

🔒 Privacy Policy
Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Virtual Galaxy Infotech Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the CyberSentinel UEBA platform.

Last updated: January 2026  |  Effective date: January 2026

Information We Collect

The CyberSentinel UEBA platform processes the following categories of data from your environment:

  • User activity logs and behavioural telemetry from monitored endpoints
  • Authentication events, logon/logoff records, and access patterns
  • Network connection metadata (source IP, destination, protocol)
  • Process execution and file access events from agents
  • Dashboard administrator account information
How We Use Your Data

Data collected by the platform is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Detecting anomalous and potentially malicious user behaviour
  • Generating risk scores, verdicts, and security alerts
  • Producing audit trails and compliance reports
  • Improving detection algorithms and platform performance

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as it is needed for detection, investigation, and audit. All retention windows are configurable per deployment; the defaults are:

  • Alert records: archived daily and retained for 120 days, then automatically purged (configurable).
  • Raw event logs: held in a rolling buffer and flushed every ~3 days or once a size cap is reached (configurable).
  • User risk profiles & behavioural models: retained while the associated endpoint/agent remains active, and pruned when it is decommissioned.
Data Security

All data processed by CyberSentinel UEBA stays within your organisation's own infrastructure — it is never transmitted to Virtual Galaxy or any third party. IP geolocation is performed entirely on-premises against a local database, with no external lookups. Data is protected by your environment's encryption in transit and at rest and by role-based access controls on the dashboard. CyberSentinel UEBA is developed and maintained by an ISO 9001:2015, ISO/IEC 27001:2013 (information security management) and ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 (IT service management) certified organisation.
